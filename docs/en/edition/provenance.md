# Copyright & Provenance

The digital typeset edition of Macdonell's *A Vedic Grammar for Students* combines an open public-domain heritage with state-of-the-art open-access practices.

---

## 🏛️ Historical Work

- **Author**: Arthur Anthony Macdonell (1854–1930), Boden Professor of Sanskrit at Oxford.
- **Title**: *A Vedic Grammar for Students* (including a chapter on syntax and three appendices: list of verbs, metre, accent).
- **Publisher**: Oxford University Press / Clarendon Press, London & Oxford, 1916.
- **Copyright Status**: Arthur Anthony Macdonell passed away in 1930. Under copyright law in the European Union, Switzerland, and the United States (70 years *post mortem auctoris* / works published prior to 1928), the historical edition is in the **Public Domain worldwide**.

---

## 🛠️ Digital Typesetting & TEI Encoding

Digitization, optical reconstruction, TEI-P5 structuring, and typographic vector typesetting were executed through the **AlexandriaSandwich Pipeline** for the **Birchville Foundation**.

### Core Components
1. **Typst Typesetting Engine**: High-precision type area with automated running headers and register pagination.
2. **TEI-P5 XML**: Standardized archival master format marking roots, citations, and structural morphology.
3. **EPUB 3**: Reflowable e-book format for reading on handheld devices.
4. **PyMuPDF Outline Pipeline**: Script-driven injection of the 81-step outline tree and Roman/Arabic page labels.
